3. Check that all chapters are visible

### **Step 3: Print to PDF** (5 minutes)
1. Press `Ctrl+P` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+P` (Mac)
2. Set print settings:
   - Destination: Save as PDF
   - Margins: None or Minimal
   - Page size: A4
   - Orientation: Portrait
   - Background graphics: ☐ Unchecked
3. Click "Save"
4. Choose filename and location
5. Click "Save" to complete PDF export

### **Step 4: Submit** (2 minutes)
- Submit your PDF to your institution following their submission guidelines
